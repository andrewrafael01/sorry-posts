# What the user's reference videos actually do (9 TikToks, transcribed + frame-sheeted)

## The Drake president reel (the one to copy)
Voice: one fast storyteller, ~60 s, one continuous 3D room (Oval Office) + a few cutaways (Toronto skyline, CN Tower, plane).
Every joke is REAL Drake lore, so it is funny to anyone who knows him, and each line sets up the next:
- "One problem? He's Canadian." -> proves he's American by dropping a nine-minute diss track about Canada -> Congress approves it in four minutes.
- Red nuclear phone rings, he just dances -> "The Pentagon now calls it the Hotline Bling Doctrine." (song title turned into policy)
- Ex calls about a parking ticket -> national day of mourning, flags half-mast, four-paragraph caption about growth (Drake's real emo-caption brand)
- Bets the entire national budget on the Raptors live on stream -> they lose -> "The Drake curse is now a federal agency." (real Drake-betting-curse meme)
- 11:59 PM, unknown number, it's Kendrick, says nothing, plays one beat -> Drake resigns, flies home to Toronto, Canada sends him back.
- Ends: "Next president gets picked by our followers. Follow, then comment the name." (series hook, comments = growth)
Screen: big white bold word-by-word captions in the middle, a label per beat ("Call #1 3:00 AM", "Call #2", "Same Night", "Call #3 11:59 PM"), cutaway props that carry the joke (fake post card with the caption, bet slip "ALL IN $7 TRILLION", clock at 11:59, Toronto sign).
RULE: a reference is only a joke if the viewer already knows the thing AND the line says the thing out loud (Raptors, Kendrick, Hotline Bling, Toronto). "Drake hugs everyone / cries" is not Drake lore. Names like Dave/Gary/Dylan with no setup are noise.

## What my reels did wrong
1. Premises were not tied to anything the viewer knows (Drake "became a landlord/doctor/bus driver" = generic nice guy). No lore, no callbacks, no escalation chain.
2. Random named extras (Dave, Gary, Kevin, Dylan) with no setup/payoff.
3. Labels instead of a story: 2-word beats ("Wifi.", "He's crying.") do not carry a joke; the ref uses full sentences with a turn in each.
4. No live number that climbs, no recurring location, no ending line that drives follows/comments.
5. Faces/crowds too small to read; ref keeps ONE hero character large in frame.
