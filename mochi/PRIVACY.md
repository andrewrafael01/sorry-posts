# Privacy policy: Mochi and Toast

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Mochi and Toast (Instagram: [@mochiandtoastworld](https://www.instagram.com/mochiandtoastworld/)) is an original comic. The Meta app "Sorry Posting" is a private tool its creator uses to publish our own posts to our own Instagram account and to reply to comments left on those posts. It is not offered to the public, and nobody signs in to it except the account owner.

## What we collect

When you comment on one of our posts, Instagram's API gives the app:

- the comment's ID and text
- your Instagram username
- the ID of the post you commented on
- the time of the comment

We store these in our database so the app replies to each comment once and never twice. We also store the reply we sent and whether it went through.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, messages, followers or anything else from your account.

## How we use it

Only to reply to your comment and to avoid replying twice. We don't sell it, share it, use it for ads, or combine it with any other data.

## Where it's kept and for how long

Comment records sit in a private database (Supabase) that only the account owner can access. We delete them after 90 days.

## Deleting your data

Send a direct message to [@mochiandtoastworld](https://www.instagram.com/mochiandtoastworld/) saying "delete my data". We'll delete every record tied to your username within 30 days and reply to confirm. Deleting your comment on Instagram also stops us from replying to it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
